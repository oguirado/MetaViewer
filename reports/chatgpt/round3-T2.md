### Why these are distinct findings

T2-R1 is a missed abnormal-read cancellation edge; T2-R2 prevents the reader from seeing a control message at all. Fixing either does not fix the other. The wait cycle in T2-R1 uses a handler that honors cancellation, so it does not depend on an arbitrary uncooperative implementation.

T2-R3 exceeds one publisher's configured budget when its eviction floor makes admission infeasible. T2-R4 gives each independently constructed publisher a fresh “global” budget. A correct per-publisher cap still does not bound the sum across roots. The arithmetic in both rows is [DERIVED] from three bytes per RGB24 pixel and the configured caps.

T2-R7 concerns independently confirmed absence only. A failed destroy also writes the state “lost” before verified removal at [daemon-adapter/internal/capsule/manager.go:553-557](https://github.com/oguirado/MetaViewer/blob/ae9361bb3d645eee1976ec8d739dc358b572561b/daemon-adapter/internal/capsule/manager.go#L553-L557). Such uncertain holders must retain capacity. Historical visibility in list and live native capacity are different responsibilities; simply ignoring every lost binding would create an admission hole.

T2-R9 has two admissible schedules: direct concurrent Manager calls can complete destroy while Open is pending; in the shipped runtime, the independent liveness monitor can mark lost before waiting for the stream work mutex to emit. In both cases the late Open commits without revalidating ownership. This differs from T2-R10, where a properly registered view's own cleanup omits its descendant group.

T2-R11 is established by the caller contract at [daemon-adapter/internal/record/record.go:2-4](https://github.com/oguirado/MetaViewer/blob/ae9361bb3d645eee1976ec8d739dc358b572561b/daemon-adapter/internal/record/record.go#L2-L4) and the complete daemon integration: the only subsequent-capture entry point is Poll, and the sole integration call is immediately after start. The recording library being pull-driven is intentional; failure to supply its scheduler is the defect. T2-R12 separately prevents an already-active record from completing after surface unregister.

T2-R14 is a stat/use race on the authorized destination basename. The portable no-follow helper correctly stats the entry without following a symlink; that does not make a later name-based unlink conditional on the same inode. No arbitrary-path deletion or privilege escalation is asserted.

### Q2: a liveness rule with an explicit observation model

**[DERIVED; E2/E5/E10]** Current code counts only typed unavailable results from a bounded frame probe and resets on unknown outcomes; it does not implement an SPRT. The following is an optional alternative, not a description of shipped behavior.

Assume conditionally independent conclusive replies X_i in {0,1}, with 1 meaning unavailable, and two simple hypotheses:

\[
H_0:\Pr(X_i=1)=p_0=0.01,\qquad
H_1:\Pr(X_i=1)=p_1=0.99.
\]

Define the exact likelihood ratio

\[
L_n=\prod_{i=1}^{n}
\left(\frac{p_1}{p_0}\right)^{X_i}
\left(\frac{1-p_1}{1-p_0}\right)^{1-X_i}.
\]

For conservative exact error budgets, declare lost when L_n>=1/alpha and alive when L_n<=beta; otherwise remain undecided. For any first-hit loss path, its probability under H0 is its H1 probability divided by L_n, hence at most alpha times that probability. First-hit paths are disjoint, so summing proves false-loss probability at most alpha. The symmetric calculation gives false-alive probability at most beta. This proof uses these conservative thresholds rather than treating approximate Wald boundaries as exact in the presence of overshoot.

Set alpha=10^-4 and beta=0.01. Each unavailable result multiplies L by 99 and each conclusive success divides it by 99. Two consecutive misses give 9801<10000; three give 970299>10000. Two consecutive successes cross the alive boundary. Unknown replies must not produce a terminal verdict; no finite decision time follows if conclusive evidence stops arriving.

**Property oracle:** enumerate binary prefixes through length12 using exact rational likelihoods, stop each at its first boundary and sum probabilities under both hypotheses. Require false-loss <= 10^-4, false-alive <= 0.01 and correct behavior for unknown replies between observations. The finite enumeration is a regression check; the disjoint-path argument establishes the bound beyond length12 under the model.

These budgets cover one episode. For repeated attempts, use a session-wide spending rule such as alpha_j=alpha_session/[j(j+1)]; its telescoping sum is alpha_session. Otherwise repeated independent “small risk” episodes can accumulate substantial false-loss risk. A hostile native program can deliberately lie, so the Bernoulli model is not a security proof and cannot replace the required identity/removal evidence.

### Q4: retention feasibility, occupancy and drops

**[DERIVED; E5/E6/E8/E9]** For equal frame size b, publication interval P and a hard minimum lifetime T, the unexpired half-open window (t-T,t] contains ceil(T/P) frames in the steady periodic case. Together with the two-generation floor, necessary capacity is

\[
B_s\ge b\max(2,\lceil T/P\rceil),\qquad
K_s\ge\max(2,\lceil T/P\rceil).
\]

Independent surfaces' byte requirements add. Even without a time guarantee, established floor feasibility requires sum_s 2b_s <= B_total. For variable future frames bounded by F_s, reserving 2F_s per surface is sufficient for the two-frame floor, but not for an arbitrary-rate four-second window.

At 1280x900, RGB24 contains3,456,000 bytes per frame. With P=100ms and T=4s:

- Forty frames require 138,240,000 bytes =131.8359375 MiB.
- A128 MiB cap fits floor(134217728/3456000)=38 frames.
- The 39th publication at 3.8s after the first needs eviction or refusal while that first frame is younger than 4s.
- Four such surfaces require 527.34375 MiB, above512 MiB.
- At 1280x720, forty RGB24 frames require 110,592,000 bytes and fit within128 MiB.

Thus unconditional four-second retention, successful 10Hz admission of continuously changed 1280x900 frames and a 128 MiB cap cannot all be true. The existing cap-priority test means the implemented four-second value is conditional under pressure; this is a contract qualification, not an additional finding for intentional early eviction. If a hard four seconds is required without enlarging that cap, the maximum steady changed-publication rate is38/4=9.5Hz, or an interval of at least4/38s, approximately 105.263ms. Other sizes and forced publications must enter the same admission accounting.

T2-R3 remains a defect under either priority: when all protected floors consume the budget, the next publication must refuse rather than exceed the cap. T2-R4 remains a defect because root-specific budgets do not bound their sum.

**Task/failure state:** the induction over admission and terminal removal gives card(T)<=S and card(F)<=S; with S=16,1000 sequential distinct failures must leave zero active tasks and no more than 16 retained failures after drain. **Folded pacing and occupancy:** the scoped poll path calls emit synchronously at [daemon-adapter/internal/frames/subscriptions.go:244-248](https://github.com/oguirado/MetaViewer/blob/ae9361bb3d645eee1976ec8d739dc358b572561b/daemon-adapter/internal/frames/subscriptions.go#L244-L248) and has no separate producer-side folded-frame FIFO. For any actual bounded buffer, finite accounting gives

\[
\int_0^H Q(t)\,dt=\sum_i W_i^{[0,H]},

because each item's residence contributes one unit of occupancy during its retained interval.
